AI approach · কৃত্রিম বুদ্ধিমত্তার ভূমিকা

AI for perception.
Rules and humans for judgement.

এআই ছবি পড়ে; সিদ্ধান্ত নেয় নিয়ম আর মানুষ।

What models do

  • Read cut strips and Bangla prescriptions into typed fields
  • Look up unknown brands on Bangladeshi medicine sites, with a cited source
  • Write the plain-Bangla explanation of a confirmed finding

What models never do

  • Create, remove or change the severity of a safety flag
  • Show the family anything a clinician has not confirmed
  • Tell a patient to stop, start or change a medicine

Free OpenRouter models with fallback routers · a private local model (Ollama, qwen2.5:3b) for Bangla · patient names never sent to a model
